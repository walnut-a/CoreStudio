A local-first canvas for image generation.

Built on the excellent Excalidraw project, CoreStudio adds practical image generation without replacing the canvas you already know. Assets and results stay in local projects by default; choose your own models or let agents such as Codex generate images and write them back to the canvas. CoreStudio is free, open source, and customizable.

There are already plenty of image generators. Why consider CoreStudio?

Generating an image is no longer the hard part. What shapes the workflow is what happens next: where the image lives, how you keep working with it, and whether an agent can join the same project.

Most image tools live on the web, and their results do not automatically become part of a local project. Organizing and backing them up takes extra work. CoreStudio keeps projects, assets, and generated results on your device, ready to view, manage, and back up.

CoreStudio does not reinvent the canvas. Excalidraw already provides a mature, capable open-source canvas for creative and exploratory work. CoreStudio builds on it and adds only what image generation, local asset management, and agent collaboration need.

Image-generation APIs have become affordable enough for personal use. Models such as GPT Image 2 and Nano Banana 2 offer strong value when usage is moderate, while still letting you choose a provider and model and control your budget.

You can also generate images through an agent without configuring an API key in CoreStudio. CoreStudio includes comprehensive CLI support, so products such as Codex can use their own image-generation capabilities and write the results back to CoreStudio. CoreStudio adds no extra charge for this workflow; third-party subscriptions and usage limits still apply.
